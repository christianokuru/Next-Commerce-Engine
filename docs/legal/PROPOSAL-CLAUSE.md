# Proposal wording: ownership & licence

## Standard paragraph (use in every proposal)

> **Ownership & licence**
>
> Your store is built on my commerce engine: a proven platform I maintain and improve for all my clients, which is what keeps your build fast, secure and affordable.
>
> **What you own:** your storefront design and the code written specifically for you, all your content, product information and branding, and all your store data: products, orders and customers. Your domain and your accounts (hosting, database, payment provider, email) are set up in your name.
>
> **What you license:** the commerce engine, which includes the admin dashboard, the customer dashboard and the APIs. It is licensed to you permanently for this store, with the features of your chosen plan. The licence doesn't expire, and it continues even if you don't take a maintenance plan.
>
> **Source code:** [included / available for an additional ₦___ / $___]. You can host the store yourself, move it, or have another developer maintain it for you.
>
> **The licence doesn't allow** reselling or sharing the engine, using it for another store or business, or enabling features outside your plan. Upgrades to a higher plan are available at any time for the price difference, and your data carries over.
>
> Full terms are in the licence agreement signed at project start.

## Short version (for invoices, quotes or WhatsApp)

> You own your storefront design, content, data, domain and accounts. The commerce engine (admin and customer dashboards) is licensed to you permanently for this store and your chosen plan. No reselling, sharing or use for other stores. Upgrades are available any time.

## Optional: full ownership buyout (for clients who insist)

> **Full ownership option:** if you need exclusive ownership of the entire codebase, including the engine, this is available as a buyout for **₦___ / $___** on top of the project price. After a buyout, I keep the right to continue using my own pre-existing engine for other clients; you receive a copy you own outright, with no restrictions on its use.
